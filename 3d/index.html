Kill Switch Tutorial: pull the magnet, the thrusters stop

The board sits inside the hull, the magnet plug outside on the side wall. Pull the red T-handle and the power is cut 1.6 ms after the magnet leaves. Push it back in and the robot arms about 0.8 s later. The captions are in Banglish.

Before every dive

  1. Plug out, battery in: all three LEDs off.
  2. Plug in: both blue LEDs, then about 1 s later the red LIVE.
  3. Only one blue LED: do not dive.
  4. Pull the plug: LIVE off, thrusters stop.
  5. Pull it once with a luggage scale: 10–20 N.

Neither board has been built or tested yet. These numbers come from calculations and simulations. Test the first board on a bench supply with a current limit, and put a 40 A fuse in the battery + lead.

Magnetic kill switch · inside the hull

Where the Hall and reed sensors go, and how to fit them

Both sensors sit on one printed bracket, glued to the inside of the hull wall exactly behind the magnet dock. The Hall chip (A3144 from the KY-003) lies flat in a window on the dock's axis; the MC-38 reed contact sits in rails 12 mm off the axis and holds the Hall chip flat.

72 s, English voice-over. The same scene is further down without sound: scrub it or drag to look around.

Steps

  1. Glue the dock on the outside of the hull (epoxy or acrylic cement). The plug's magnet has its south pole toward the hull.
  2. On the inside of the wall, mark with a + the point where the dock's axis comes through (measure from two hull edges on both sides).
  3. Print mech/bracket.stl. Its window goes on the +, using the two centre marks on the plate edges.
  4. Hall: take the A3144 off the KY-003, or bend its legs. Put it flat in the window, printed face against the wall, legs down the channel. Join the legs to the KY-003's three sensor pads with short wires, and screw the KY-003 board onto the two posts (M2).
  5. Reed: slide the MC-38 (the wired half) into the rails, long side pointing at the axis. Its end lies over the A3144 and holds it flat. Fix it with two cable ties through the slots.
  6. Cables: twisted 3-wire from the KY-003 to J3 (1 = S, 2 = +, 3 = −), twisted 2-wire from the MC-38 to J4 (either way). Keep them at least 10 cm from the battery wires.
  7. Battery on, plug in. Slide the bracket a little until HALL and REED LEDs both stay solidly on. Mark the position, then glue the plate with epoxy.
  8. Test: plug out, both blue LEDs off. Plug in, both on, then about 1 s later the red LIVE.

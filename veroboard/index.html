Magnetic kill switch on perfboard (100 A)

The through-hole kill switch (4S–6S LiPo, 100 A, XT90 in and out) laid out on dot perfboard. Tap any pad, wire or net name to light up everything joined to it. Use the slider to zoom and drag to move. Parts, holes and nets come from the KiCad board ks_tht.kicad_pcb (make_vero_data.py), so the wiring matches the PCB exactly.

100 A must never flow through perfboard pads, strips or thin wire. The thick copper lines are 8 AWG bare copper bus wires (3.3 mm), soldered straight to the MOSFET legs and to the XT90 pins: VIN along the drains, VOUT along the sources, and the GND link from J2 − to J1 −. The perfboard only holds them in place. Put a 125–150 A fuse in the battery + lead.

Before you solder

  • Drawn for dot perfboard (one pad per hole, 2.54 mm); the board size for the chosen spacing is shown above the drawing. All wiring is on the solder side. Thin insulated wires may cross each other and the bus wires. The bare bus wires must touch nothing but their own legs and pins.
  • 100 A path: VIN (battery +), VOUT (to the ESCs) and GND carry 100 A. Use 8 AWG bare solid copper (3.3 mm, or bare earthing wire), about 35 cm in all. Lay it flat on the solder side along the drawn path. Bend each MOSFET drain leg onto the VIN wire and each source leg onto the VOUT wire, and solder them; the last hole of each drawn piece is that leg. The page keeps one free hole all round each bus wire. Afterwards, check every bus wire for solder bridges to its neighbours with a multimeter.
  • The simulated numbers above are for the PCB; with the bus wires the perfboard carries the current the same way. Without the wires the copper would take 9.3 W and Q1 alone 28 A, so the wires are not optional.
  • XT90: J1 (male, battery in) and J2 (female, to the ESCs) sit on the left edge with their bodies on the board. Drill the four pin holes out to 4 mm, push the pins through, and solder the 8 AWG bus wires straight to the pins; perfboard pads cannot carry the current. Glue or tie the connector bodies down so that plugging in does not lever the pins.
  • Fuse: put a 125–150 A fuse (ANL, MIDI or MEGA) in the battery + lead before J1. The board kills a hard short in about 0.5 ms; a long overload is the fuse's job. Use 8 AWG silicone cable to the battery and to the ESCs, as short as possible.
  • Medium lines carry a few amps for a moment: the TVS diodes D1 and D2, and the pre-charge path RK1 → Q11 → F1 → R3/R4 → RK2. Use short 20 AWG (0.5 mm²) insulated wire for them. Everything else is signal wire (30 AWG wire-wrap or thin hookup wire).
  • Pin order (printed or flat face toward you, legs down): IRFB3206 (Q1–Q8) and IRF9540N (Q11) are G-D-S. The tab is the drain, so all eight main tabs are VIN; they may touch each other (or one bar bolted across all of them) but nothing else. Q1–Q8 are turned round, with their tabs toward the VIN wire. The L7805CV lies flat on its tab: IN-GND-OUT. 2N7000 (Q12, Q16, Q17): S-G-D. 2N3906 (Q13) and 2N3904 (Q14, Q15): E-B-C. BC327 (Q18): C-B-E. In the drawing, the flat face of each TO-92 points to the bottom edge. PC817 and LM339N: pin 1 at the dot (use a socket for the LM339N).
  • D1, D2 (1.5KE33A), D3, D6 and D10 stand up with the cathode (band) lead on top. Check every diode band and electrolytic polarity against the K and + marks.
  • B0512S: check the pin order of your module against its datasheet. This board uses 1 GND, 2 Vin, 3 0 V, 4 +Vo. Its output floats on VOUT: SRC (0 V) joins VOUT only through RK2.
  • C11 must be a 3.3 µF X7R ceramic, because an electrolytic's leakage upsets the abort ramp. C6 is a 220 nF film capacitor. R37 is 47 kΩ, so Q18 can pull the gates below about 1.4 V. R21, R22, R24 and R25 should be 1 % metal film.
  • Sensors: check the KY-003 pins (S, +, −) with a multimeter, because some clones swap + and −. Check with a multimeter that the MC-38 closes when the magnet is near; listings disagree on normally open and normally closed. Mount the magnet with its south pole toward the hull, because the A3144 only reacts to a south pole on its printed face.
  • First power-up: use a current-limited bench supply (12–16 V, 0.3 A limit), no ESC, magnet out: all LEDs off. Magnet in: both blue LEDs light, then about 1 s later the red LIVE LED, and VOUT follows VIN. Pull the magnet: LIVE goes off at once. Then try the battery with one ESC, and only then full current.

Magnetic kill switch on perfboard

The through-hole kill switch (4S–6S LiPo, 30 A continuous, 60 A burst) laid out on dot perfboard. Tap any pad, wire or net name to light up everything joined to it. Use the slider to zoom and drag to move. Parts, holes and nets come from the KiCad board ks_tht.kicad_pcb (make_vero_data.py), so the wiring matches the PCB exactly.

30–60 A must never flow through perfboard pads or thin wire. The thick copper lines are bare 12 AWG (or 2.5 mm²) wire runs soldered along the pads. The MOSFET legs and the XT60 pigtail leads are soldered straight to them. Put a 40 A fuse in the battery + lead.

Before you solder

  • Drawn for dot perfboard (one pad per hole, 2.54 mm). The board size for the chosen spacing is shown above the drawing. All wiring is on the solder side. Thin insulated wires may cross each other and the runs; bare runs must touch nothing but their own pads.
  • Main current: VIN (battery +), VOUT (to the ESCs) and GND carry 30 A, 60 A in bursts. Lay bare tinned 12 AWG (or 2.5 mm²) copper wire flat on the solder side along the drawn path and solder it to every pad it passes; solder each MOSFET drain and source straight to it. The page keeps an empty hole between runs of different nets. Afterwards, check every run against its neighbours for solder bridges with a multimeter.
  • XT60: the board-mount XT60 pins do not fit 2.54 mm holes. Use XT60 pigtails with 12 AWG leads: male at J1 (battery side), female at J2 (ESC side). Lay each stripped lead 10–15 mm along the end of its run and solder it there. 12 AWG does not go through a 1 mm hole, so hold each lead down with a cable tie through two holes.
  • Fuse: put a 40 A fuse (blade or MIDI) in the battery + lead before J1. The board turns off a hard short in under 0.5 ms; a long overload is the fuse's job.
  • Medium lines (TVS diodes D1 and D2, the pre-charge path through Q5, F1, R3 and R4) see a few amps for a moment: use short 20 AWG (0.5 mm²) insulated wire. Everything else is signal wire (30 AWG wire-wrap or thin hookup wire).
  • Pin order (printed or flat face toward you, legs down): IRFB3206 and IRF9540N G-D-S; the tab is the drain, so all four main tabs are VIN. Let them touch each other but nothing else. The L7805CV lies flat on its tab: IN-GND-OUT. 2N7000: S-G-D. 2N3904 and 2N3906: E-B-C. BC327: C-B-E. In the drawing the flat face of each TO-92 points to the bottom edge. PC817 and LM339N: pin 1 at the dot (use a socket for the LM339N).
  • D3, D6 and D10 stand up with the cathode (band) lead on top. Check every diode band and both electrolytic polarities against the K and + marks.
  • B0512S: check the pin order of your module against its datasheet. This board uses 1 GND, 2 Vin, 3 0 V, 4 +Vo. Its output floats on VOUT: SRC (0 V) joins VOUT only through RK2.
  • C11 must be a 3.3 µF X7R ceramic (an electrolytic's leakage upsets the abort ramp). C6 is a 220 nF film capacitor. R21, R22, R24 and R25 should be 1 % metal film.
  • Sensors: check the KY-003 pins (S, +, −) with a multimeter, because some clones swap + and −. Check with a multimeter that the MC-38 closes when the magnet is near: listings disagree on normally open and normally closed. Mount the magnet with its south pole toward the hull, because the A3144 only reacts to a south pole on its printed face.
  • First power-up: use a current-limited bench supply (12–16 V, 0.3 A limit), no ESC, magnet out: all LEDs off. Magnet in: both blue LEDs light, then about 1 s later the red LIVE LED, and VOUT follows VIN. Pull the magnet: LIVE goes off at once. Only then try the battery with one ESC, then full current.
